# Vuzix App Store landscape

LifeLens is not the first application to bring AI or contextual assistance to
Vuzix glasses. Its differentiation is the combination of cross-domain daily
context, explicit action approval, and minimized memory.

| Existing direction | Representative app | Overlap | LifeLens distinction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Configurable enterprise vision | [Evantek Computer Vision](https://apps.vuzix.com/app/evantek-computer-vision) | Live camera and AI analytics | Personal daily moments, bounded proposals, no default streaming/archive |
| Cognitive augmentation | [Wearable Intelligence System](https://apps.vuzix.com/app/wear) | Transcription and memory tools | Explicit per-action consent and health/mobility reasoning under one contract |
| Accessibility recognition | [G-EYE](https://apps.vuzix.com/app/geye-vision-to-audio-guide) | Object/text recognition and short-lived images | Planning and follow-through rather than describing every visible object |
| Speech input | [Vuzix Voice Input](https://apps.vuzix.com/app/vuzix-voice-input) | Cloud speech-to-text | Voice is one temporary signal; only a user-approved structured commitment remains |
| Guided workflow | [Process Eye](https://apps.vuzix.com/app/processeye) | Hands-free next-step guidance | Recommendations adapt across meals, movement, calendar, and environment |

## Positioning statement

> LifeLens is the consent layer between ambient perception and everyday action.
> It does not try to recognize everything or retain a complete life log. It
> identifies one useful moment, explains one proposal, and waits for approval.

## Claims to avoid

- “The first AI app for Vuzix.” Existing computer-vision and assistant apps make
  that claim inaccurate.
- “Understands emotions” or “manages people.” The product records observable
  commitments and reflects the user's own communication behavior.
- “Accurate calorie measurement.” A monocular image supports only an estimate
  with explicit uncertainty.
- “Safe navigation.” Route context can support a suggestion but cannot certify
  physical safety.
